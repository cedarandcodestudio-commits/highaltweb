Packing guides.

Pack light, pack smart, and arrive looking effortless. Our go-to lists for every kind of journey.

Europe & Cities

The City Edit

  • Two pairs of broken-in walking shoes
  • A neutral capsule: 3 tops, 2 bottoms, 1 dress
  • A light layer or trench for evenings
  • A scarf to cover shoulders at churches
  • A crossbody bag with a zip closure
Beach & Islands

The Island Edit

  • Two swimsuits (one always drying)
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and a wide-brim hat
  • Linen cover-ups that go from beach to dinner
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Sandals plus one dressier pair
Adventure & Safari

The Wild Edit

  • Moisture-wicking base layers
  • Waterproof shell and a warm midlayer
  • Supportive hiking boots, already broken in
  • Neutral colors for game drives
  • Soft duffel if flying on small planes

Always in our carry-on.

  • Passport, plus a digital copy saved to your phone
  • Any medications in their original packaging
  • A universal adapter and portable charger
  • A change of clothes, in case your bag takes the scenic route
  • Your High Alt travel booklet with every confirmation in one place
