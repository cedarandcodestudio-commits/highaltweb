High Alt Travel was born from one belief: a woman traveling solo or with her people deserves more than a package deal.

Meet Victoria.

Hi, I'm Victoria Lamy, the founder of High Alt Travel. I started this company because I believe the best trips aren't bought off a shelf. They're designed around the person taking them.

Every journey I plan begins with a conversation about you: how you like to travel, what makes you feel most alive, and what you want to come home with. From there, I build an itinerary from scratch, tap into trusted local partners, and stay with you from the first idea to the flight home.

High Alt Travel was founded in 2026 with a simple mission: to help women see the world with confidence, comfort, and a little bit of magic.
