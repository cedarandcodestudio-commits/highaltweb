Frequently asked.

Everything you need to know about planning your journey with us. Still curious? Just ask.

How does working with High Alt Travel work?

It starts with a Dream Session, a 45-minute consultation where we get to know your travel style and must-have moments. From there, your advisor designs a custom itinerary, refines it with you until it's perfect, and handles every booking. You'll travel with a beautifully designed travel booklet and 24/7 support.

Do you only plan trips for women?

Our journeys are designed with the modern woman in mind, but we happily plan for you and your people: partners, friends, family, or a group celebration.

How far in advance should I start planning?

For international trips, we recommend reaching out 6 to 12 months ahead, especially for peak seasons, popular hotels, or special occasions. We can sometimes work on shorter timelines, so it's always worth asking.

How much does it cost?

Every trip is different, so pricing depends on your destination, length of stay, and the experiences you choose. We'll walk you through everything transparently during your Dream Session, before you commit to anything.

Where do you plan trips to?

Almost anywhere. Browse our destinations in Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas, and Oceania, or tell us about somewhere new.

What happens if something goes wrong while I'm traveling?

You'll have 24/7 support throughout your trip. If a flight is delayed or plans change, we'll help you rebook and adjust so you can keep enjoying your journey.

Do I need travel insurance?

We strongly recommend it for every trip. See our travel insurance guide for what to look for.

Do you help with visas?

We'll flag entry requirements for your itinerary and point you to the right official resources. See our visa info page to get started.

How do I stay in the loop?

Join The Altitude Edit, our Friday newsletter, and follow along on Instagram and our travel journal.
