Travel insurance.

The best trips are the ones you can enjoy without worry. Here's why we recommend insurance for every journey.

Why it matters.

Luxury travel often involves significant prepaid, non-refundable costs. A good policy protects that investment and, more importantly, protects you if something goes wrong far from home.

Coverage

Trip Cancellation

Reimburses prepaid, non-refundable costs if you have to cancel or cut your trip short for a covered reason, such as illness or a family emergency.

Coverage

Medical & Evacuation

Your home health plan may not cover you abroad. Look for strong emergency medical limits and medical evacuation, especially for remote destinations.

Upgrade

Cancel for Any Reason

An optional upgrade that lets you cancel for reasons a standard policy won't cover, usually for partial reimbursement. It typically must be bought soon after your first trip payment.

Before you buy.

  • Buy early. Some benefits, like pre-existing condition waivers, are only available if you purchase within a set window after your initial deposit.
  • Insure the full trip cost. Include flights, hotels, tours, and any other prepaid, non-refundable expenses.
  • Read the exclusions. Check how the policy treats adventure activities, pre-existing conditions, and travel advisories.
  • Save the emergency number. Keep your policy number and 24/7 assistance line on your phone and in your travel booklet.

This page is general information, not insurance advice. Coverage varies by provider and policy, so review the terms carefully. Your advisor is happy to talk through options when you book.
